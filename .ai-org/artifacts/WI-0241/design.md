## Minimal instrument change

Introduce a separate version, `continuity-incremental-approved/v1`, with exactly
two compact subjects in stable/changed-spec order. Keep the existing default
four-subject and instruction-comparison six-subject versions unchanged. Reject
mixed modes, additional/duplicate/swapped conditions, ordinary/previous variants,
wrong roots and forged envelopes. Do not create an arbitrary subject-selection
or resume switch. Reuse the same model request, sandbox, oracle, usage ledger,
cleanup, canary and typed continuation mechanisms.

Proposed limits retain the instrument's existing Terra medium, 100,000 Operational
Tokens and eight minutes per subject; total 200,000 and twenty minutes (sixteen
minutes actor ceilings plus four minutes existing per-subject setup allowance).
These are conservative inherited stop ceilings, not predictions or evidence that
compact will finish: the old Temple subject exceeded 100,000. There is no observed
compact completion from which to infer a larger safe cap. If a cap is reached,
preserve censoring and stop under the existing policy; do not repair/retry mid-run.
Changing these proposed limits requires separately reviewed instrument changes.

Preparation creates a new exclusive lab. It does not resume or alter WI-0239's lab.
Freeze runtime/instrument/schema/configuration/initial seed digests and preserve
consumed/run/seal exclusivity. Compare old pre-work seeds and product/test/spec
blobs read-only; do not use an old completed checkout as the new starting point.
Generated IDs, timestamps and installation records may differ and must be labeled.
