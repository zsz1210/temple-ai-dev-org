Effective turn effort or immutable model revision stays unknown unless observed. Keep `gpt-5.6-terra` medium and `gpt-6-astra` medium as the requested comparison categories, subject to actual provider acknowledgement and governing approval.

Report product/oracle acceptance, exact handoff, fresh Verifier outcome, human intervention and rework, observed stage wall time, non-cached input plus output Tokens, cached/total Tokens, and command output bytes separately. Byte reduction is a mechanism measurement, not causal Token attribution. Per-operation Token deltas require timestamped cumulative usage observations and uncertainty handling; do not fabricate them from output size.

Retain the original 80,000 observed operational Token / six-minute per-stage envelope as a **design reference only**, not new permission. Define and approve the new matrix's exact number of stages and aggregate envelope after the generation-free rehearsal. A consumed one-shot matrix approval cannot authorize the next matrix. No reset, purchase, fallback or retry is implied.

One matched pair may screen a change but cannot establish a model ranking or general framework advantage. Counterbalanced repetitions and task shapes that exercise changing requirements, recovery or coordination are needed before positioning claims. Small tasks where ordinary delivery already succeeds should measure Temple's additional cost directly; do not add complexity merely to favor Temple.
