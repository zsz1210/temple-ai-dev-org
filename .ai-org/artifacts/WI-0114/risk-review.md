# WI-0114 risk review

Primary risks are overwriting a project-owned Claude file, implying authority from successful bootstrap, importing collided Work Item state, using an invalid revision, and merging stale-main evidence.

Mitigations are byte preservation, fail-closed pending merge, false authority flags, product-only transplant, Git-resolved revisions, a coordinated visible branch reservation, exact-revision evidence, final remote-main ancestry check, and distinct Independent QA.
