# WI-0102 approved scope

## Outcome

Turn the retained adoption evidence into one honest Wave 1 baseline, then fill the smallest local gap with a reproducible brownfield rehearsal. The result should tell a future User Guide which workflows are demonstrated, which are only simulated, and which still require another person or environment.

## Reused evidence

- FlowDeck is the retained greenfield product-definition and first-lifecycle pilot.
- IdeaDock is the retained cold-task recovery and parallel-delivery pilot.
- AiPet is the retained data-bearing backup, restore, and forward-upgrade rehearsal.
- WI-0097 and WI-0098 are the adaptive repository-workflow implementation and verification evidence.

Reusing these records does not broaden their claims. Every limitation remains attached to its source.

## New local rehearsal

Create an isolated temporary Git repository that already contains product source, human-facing documentation, requirements, a contribution policy, and history. Initialize Temple from the repository-pinned candidate, confirm the existing repository integration policy, make one bounded application change, and exercise the complete organizational lifecycle with exact revisions and distinct Developer and Independent QA identities.

The test must record:

- elapsed time by major stage;
- the exact pre-existing file digests before and after adoption;
- the paths changed by Temple initialization and by the bounded product change;
- application test results, Doctor results, and lifecycle result;
- whether model generation, external writes, Docker, deployment, publication, or release occurred; and
- limits that prevent the fixture from being presented as independent-human or enterprise evidence.

## Stop boundary

Stop after the evidence matrix and deterministic rehearsal pass or produce a truthful no-go record if they do not. Do not continue the sample product, modify a real product repository, launch a model task, install infrastructure, or advance the public Alpha.

## Roles

- Engineering Manager: Mog (`agent-mog`) owns experiment boundaries and evidence normalization.

- Developer: Rikku (`agent-rikku`) implements the deterministic test and result capture.
- Quality Evaluator and Independent QA: Lulu (`agent-lulu`) evaluates and independently reproduces the exact candidate.
