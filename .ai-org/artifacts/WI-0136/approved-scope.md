# WI-0136 approved scope

## Decision question

For one realistic, bounded change spanning four service repositories, does Temple improve recovery, task-boundary quality, contract convergence, correctness, rework, or Human coordination enough to justify its measured context, Token, time, and artifact overhead when compared with a competent minimal responsible workflow?

## Included

- One synthetic commerce change across Gateway, Catalog, Orders, and Notifications repositories.
- Two matched arms: `minimal-responsible` and `temple`.
- One consequential design turn per arm, three isolated implementation slices per arm, one fresh integration/recovery turn per arm, and one arm-blind final evaluator.
- The same Sol xhigh design route, Terra medium delivery route, and Sol xhigh evaluator route for both arms.
- Objective public and held-out compatibility tests, arm-neutral scoring, exact Provider usage, elapsed time, changed-line retention, intervention events, and artifact footprint.
- Local-only execution with network disabled inside model turns.

## Excluded

- Production services, real customer data, external trackers, hosted CI, deployment, release, publication, and repository-visibility changes.
- Credits purchase, automatic refill, reset redemption, retry, model fallback, reroute acceptance, or continuation after a stopped run.
- Automatic changes to Temple's model-routing or workflow defaults.
- Statistical or general product-superiority claims from one scenario.

## Account boundary

Fixture generation and no-generation Provider handshakes are allowed before live approval. The first generation turn is blocked until a machine-readable approval matches the frozen protocol digest, model route, turn count, operational Token ceiling, wall-clock ceiling, and zero-retry/fallback/payment conditions.

## Acceptance reference

The five acceptance criteria stored on `WI-0136` are authoritative. A completed harness without a live approved run is preparation evidence only, not an effectiveness result.
