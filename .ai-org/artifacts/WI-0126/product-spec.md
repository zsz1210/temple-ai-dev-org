# WI-0126 roadmap content contract

## Required reader questions

Each roadmap must answer, in this order:

1. What long-term outcome is Temple pursuing?
2. What can the repository evidence support today?
3. What single mission is active now?
4. How will the project know that mission succeeded?
5. What comes next, with visibly lower certainty?
6. What is optional, paused, or explicitly not on the critical path?
7. Where can a reader find detailed delivery and validation records?

## Information architecture

- **Vision:** one stable outcome, independent of the current release number.
- **How to read this roadmap:** outcomes and evidence here; tasks in Work Items; experiments in validation records; release gates in release readiness.
- **Evidence-backed current position:** concise capability and limitation table.
- **Now — make the core path dependable:** one mission with measurable exit signals.
- **Next — qualify real adoption:** greenfield, brownfield, multi-repository, collaboration, and matched comparison outcomes.
- **Later — expand safely:** Provider execution, high-assurance and enterprise qualification, optional retrieval, and public distribution.
- **Optional tools:** Management Console and Usage observation stay useful but are never required for the core path.
- **Explicitly paused:** public release remains deferred until the owner reopens it.

## Evidence rules

- `done` means the bounded Work Item closed with its required evidence; it does not generalize beyond that scope.
- `no-go` and `inconclusive` are completed experimental outcomes, not active delivery blockers and not success claims.
- Missing Provider observation is `unknown`, never zero.
- A local, synthetic, simulated, or single-user result cannot become a real enterprise or comparative claim.
- Cross-comparison is **started but not qualified**: Wave 5A and 5B supplied mechanism and overhead evidence, while the comparison result remains insufficient.
- Execution Route is deterministic and read-only. It cannot launch a model or apply a recommendation.

## Current mission exit signals

The core-path mission is complete only when repository evidence shows:

- a person or Agent can complete one documented path from project initialization through Work Item closeout without the Management Console;
- a fresh task can recover the current state and next action from repository evidence rather than prior chat;
- Lean, Standard, and High-Assurance profile selection and escalation are understandable and bounded;
- safe parallel work, handoff, Independent QA, and recovery remain enforceable;
- the same path is demonstrated in at least one greenfield and one existing-project setting; and
- observed friction, interventions, rework, elapsed time, and resource usage are reported as measured or unknown, with no invented savings claim.

## Language contract

English is the canonical structural version. Japanese and Traditional Chinese keep the same sections, evidence states, and links, but use natural technical prose rather than sentence-by-sentence translation.
