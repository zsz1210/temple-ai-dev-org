# Work order — WI-0118

Temple has accumulated enough validation output to stop treating additional runs as progress by themselves. This Work Item turns the retained evidence into a smaller, adaptive operating model and a decision-useful next benchmark.

## Outcome

Deliver four connected improvements:

1. a source-linked retrospective that explains what the existing evidence supports, what it contradicts, and what remains unknown;
2. measurable Lean, Standard, and High-Assurance workflow profiles, with escalation based on risk and observed uncertainty rather than project size labels alone;
3. a terminal historical outcome for completed no-go or inconclusive experiments that is distinct from an actionable delivery blocker; and
4. a rehearsed specification for a representative multi-Agent, multi-repository microservice comparison.

## Model policy for this Work Item

- Use GPT-5.6 Sol with xhigh reasoning for consequential evidence interpretation, product boundaries, architecture, and final independent evaluation.
- Use GPT-5.6 Terra for ordinary implementation, debugging, documentation integration, and focused tests.
- Use GPT-5.6 Luna only for explicitly bounded mechanical work where the task contract is already stable.
- Apply the same routing to both arms of any future comparison. Model choice must not become the experimental variable unless a separate experiment explicitly studies it.

These are routing guidelines, not an automatic model selector. Unsupported statistical thresholds must not be introduced.

## Evidence and implementation boundary

- Reuse retained repository evidence from WI-0107, WI-0108, WI-0110, WI-0112, and WI-0117.
- Treat objective acceptance, operational Tokens, elapsed time, retries, human intervention, and protocol failures as separate measurements.
- Do not infer billed cost, statistical superiority, or general productivity savings from the current sample.
- Do not launch another Provider-backed comparison during this Work Item. The next benchmark must pass a pure-local schema, fixture, and scoring rehearsal first.
- Keep active delivery blockers visible and actionable. Move concluded no-go or inconclusive experiment outcomes to historical reporting without rewriting their evidence.
- Preserve Developer and Independent QA separation wherever a selected profile requires Independent QA.

## Stop condition

Stop after the implementation, focused regression tests, full repository verification, and revision-matched Independent QA evidence are complete. A live model-backed microservice experiment, release, publication, deployment, and external service mutation remain separate decisions.
