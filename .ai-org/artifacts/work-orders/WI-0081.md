# Work order — WI-0081

## Outcome

Implement the accepted WI-0077 Management Console direction in the production Temple Workspace renderer and populate it exclusively from the real control-plane snapshot and event stream.

## Authorized scope

- Apply the proposal's human-facing information architecture, visual hierarchy, responsive shell, and interaction language to `src/control-plane-dashboard.mjs`.
- Render Overview, Work, Team, Usage, System, and History from the current `/api/v1/snapshot` contract.
- Preserve the existing coalesced `/api/v1/events` refresh path.
- Keep the LAN and Tailscale viewers redacted, read-only, and GET-only.
- Keep Human Inbox and Agent Commands loopback-only and preserve their existing safety behavior.
- Add focused renderer and private-view regression coverage.

## Non-goals

- Do not copy the proposal's hardcoded Work Items, Agents, Token totals, scenarios, dates, or authority claims into production.
- Do not infer an execution state, impediment state, Human authority, cost, savings, model quality, or routing recommendation that the snapshot does not provide.
- Do not change the Observer, provider protocol, snapshot schema, mutation authority, release state, repository visibility, or external services.
