## Risk review

- Retention must never recursively delete an unresolved or implicit path.
- Audit export must reapply redaction and must not retain prompts, responses, hidden reasoning, credentials, or provider payload bodies.
- Provider incompatibility must degrade to a bounded diagnostic without reconnect churn or invented usage.
- A portfolio must never mutate a participant repository or treat stale, missing, invalid, or identity-mismatched state as complete.
- Local simulation is not evidence of real multi-human, multi-machine, protected-branch, regulated, production, or cost behavior.

## Stop condition

Stop after a clean exact candidate has reproducible local evidence and the Phase 4 document distinguishes closed local capabilities from explicitly retained external validation. Do not push, publish, deploy, alter an external tracker, change a model, incur spending, or claim unsupported efficiency improvements.

## Rollback

Revert the Phase 4 integration commits. Child Work Item history and validation artifacts remain available as repository evidence.
