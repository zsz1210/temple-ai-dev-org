# Work Order — WI-0010

## Outcome

Ship the first Phase 4B foundation: deterministic adversarial policy evaluation and explainable usage-driver attribution. The implementation must provide evidence before any automatic model routing, cost claim, or lifecycle authority expansion.

## Deliverables

1. Install a managed, versioned adversarial scenario catalog covering false completion, wrong revision, self-approval, unauthorized external action, stale scope, context loss, and noisy notifications.
2. Add a read-only `evaluation run` command that validates a project-owned observation fixture, compares it with the catalog, and produces a scorecard without mutating lifecycle state.
3. Distinguish `prevented`, `detected`, `recovered`, `unknown`, and `escaped`; treat missing and unknown scenarios as incomplete and an escaped invariant as failed.
4. Add representative Solo, Collaborative, and High-Assurance fixtures plus negative cases that prove escaped, missing, side-effect, and evidence violations fail closed.
5. Extend provider-reported usage events with provable attribution and explicit `unknown` values for unavailable dimensions.
6. Add a read-only `usage report` command that aggregates numeric usage deltas by Work Item, Position, stage, task, attempt, provider, model, and outcome; optionally write a generated baseline view.
7. Keep monetary cost unknown without a versioned, user-approved price source and keep automatic routing disabled.
8. Publish the capability as the next alpha, update the roadmap and operating contract, and retain the ten-real-Work-Item and longitudinal baseline requirements.

## Policy evaluation contract

- The managed catalog defines expected outcomes, required invariant checks, allowed side effects, required evidence labels, and cleanup boundaries.
- A fixture is an observation record, not authority and not proof of a live production exercise.
- A passing score requires every profile-applicable scenario, an acceptable outcome, all required invariant checks, every required evidence label, and no undeclared side effect.
- `unknown` and missing cases are incomplete rather than silently passing.
- `escaped`, failed invariants, undeclared side effects, and missing evidence are failures.
- The scorecard includes outcome counts, gate integrity, revision correctness, separation of duties, external authority, context recovery, recovery steps, rework, human intervention, and notification precision.

## Usage attribution contract

- Prefer provider-reported usage and use the provider's last-turn delta for aggregation rather than summing cumulative totals.
- Carry project, Work Item, Position, current observed lifecycle stage, task, provider thread/turn, attempt, provider, model, model version, reasoning configuration, service tier, Context Capsule digest, capability-set digest, provenance, quality, and outcome.
- Missing values remain `null` and are listed under `missing_dimensions`; they are never converted to zero or inferred from display names.
- Reconciled history labels its lifecycle stage as the current canonical stage at observation time, not historical truth.
- Reports retain only bounded identifiers and numbers. They do not retain prompts, hidden reasoning, source bodies, commands, diffs, tool payloads, or credentials.
- Cost stays `unknown`; recommendations and automatic switching remain disabled.

## Verification

- New Phase 4B module and CLI tests.
- Existing Control Plane privacy, replay, disconnect, and lifecycle tests.
- Schema catalog and managed-overlay checks.
- Full `npm run verify` at the exact candidate revision.
- Independent QA from a clean detached worktree.

## Exclusions and retained evidence

- No automatic model selection, provider switching, price lookup, spending action, external notification, tracker write, deployment, or release.
- No claim of Token savings, cost savings, elapsed-time reduction, or lower rework until a baseline exists.
- No claim that fixture results replace live exercises or regulated organizational acceptance.
- Phase 4B exit remains open until at least ten real Work Items across varied task shapes and representative profiles provide longitudinal evidence.

## Rollback

Revert the candidate commit. Generated policy scorecards and usage baseline views are disposable; canonical Work Items, evidence, approvals, and provider journals retain their existing authority and privacy boundaries.
