A terminal Work Item must use its top-level `tested_revision` as the current exact-revision authority. Older evidence remains visible as immutable historical audit data, but revision-stale evidence attached to a terminal Work Item must not create current attention or a firing `stale-evidence` condition. - Preserve the `stale` classification and count on every evidence record so historical measurements and audit inspection remain truthful.
- Treat staleness as actionable only while the owning Work Item is nonterminal. Apply that boundary consistently to Observer attention and control-plane conditions.
- Add regression coverage for terminal exact-revision selection, retained historical classification, suppressed terminal attention, and unchanged nonterminal alerting.
- Document the distinction between evidence history and current operational attention.

## Verification

## Risk review and rollback
