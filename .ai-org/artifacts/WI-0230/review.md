## Challenges and conclusions

- Fact parity is explicit: both arms receive the same product history, current
  authority, old/current facts, competent handoff and stated changed requirement.
  An arm-neutral inventory must check parity before dispatch. The treatment is
  representation and mandatory administration, not concealed information.
- Stable partial work and approved requirement change are separate deterministic
  checkpoints. Completed behavior is protected; their incidence is not presented
  as a measured real-world interruption distribution.
- The endpoint is Build submission plus exact candidate/handoff. Later Verifier,
  human review and delivery costs are explicitly excluded, not counted as zero.
  Preparation and takeover costs are separately measured and combined only where
  compatible. This cannot support a whole-delivery efficiency claim.
- Objective acceptance and negative controls cover correct completion, stale
  threshold and damage to completed behavior in both arms. Protected scope and
  allowed changes are predeclared; good prose or Temple administration cannot
  override a failing product oracle. Actual fixture/oracle implementation remains
  a prerequisite, not completed evidence.
- Eight proposed subjects provide order coverage across two states, not statistical
  confidence, reliability rates or eight full delivery cycles. All attempts and
  partial costs remain visible; no retry, replacement or automatic extension.
- Useful-effect threshold, permissible resource tradeoff, exact budget/duration,
  privacy contract and launch authorization remain deliberately unset. This is
  acceptable for the approved design-only scope and a hard prelaunch boundary,
  not hidden authorization. No current model availability or optimality is certified.

No concrete design blocker was found. Before implementation or launch, a separate
authorized scope must define exact fixture semantics and oracle, prove offline
negative controls/isolation, and resolve the useful-effect and budget choices.
The proposal itself cannot choose these later by implication or reuse an old cap.
