# WI-0106 product specification

## Problem

Temple has local adoption, coordination, microservice, and operating-boundary evidence, but it still has no controlled measurement showing whether Temple changes delivery quality or resource use relative to a credible minimal process. Natural project history cannot answer that question because task difficulty, model, context, and workflow differ.

The framework also has a deterministic matched-model evaluator, but no real project-owned matched evaluation source. Mixing these two questions would make any result impossible to interpret.

## Outcome

Provide an executable, staged experiment design that can answer two distinct questions:

1. **Process question:** for the same bounded engineering case and the same model profile, how does Temple differ from a minimal but responsible workflow?
2. **Model question:** inside the Temple workflow and one exact task shape, does a lower-resource GPT-5.6 profile retain the required quality relative to the current baseline profile?

## Product rules

- The two questions use separate datasets and conclusions.
- The minimal arm keeps the same product brief, acceptance tests, safety restrictions, and Independent QA. It is not an intentionally weak straw baseline.
- Missing usage, failed correlation, model drift, or a changed input invalidates the pair; it never becomes zero.
- Quality is evaluated before Token, latency, or Credits differences.
- Two feasibility cases produce diagnostic data only. They cannot support a savings, superiority, model-routing, or enterprise-readiness claim.
- A qualified follow-on sample size is chosen from pilot variance and the predeclared statistical contract, not from a convenient passing result.
- No result enables automatic routing, external release, or a marketing claim by itself.

## Acceptance

- Every intervention, controlled variable, metric, exclusion, and stop condition is explicit.
- The proposed pilot can be executed without prior-chat context and without company or production data.
- The resource proposal cites its empirical inputs, calculation, uncertainty, and non-guarantees.
- The next approval request names a bounded number of turns and states how additional payment is prevented or authorized.
