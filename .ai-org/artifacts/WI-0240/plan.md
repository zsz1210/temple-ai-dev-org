## Incremental experiment design, not launch authority

Question: does the compact contract merit further investigation on these same two
takeover conditions? This is an engineering screen, not a savings claim.

1. Retrieve this inventory and the active scoped Practice. Use existing ordinary
   and previous-Temple rows as explicitly historical references, separated by
   requirement condition and measurement contract. Do not compute a pooled mean
   across WI-0234/0236/0239 or turn a stopped row into completed-delivery cost.
2. The default missing execution set is **two compact subjects**, stable then
   changed, using verified pre-work seeds and fresh sessions. Ordinary/old Temple
   are not automatically rerun. A fresh control requires an explicit unresolved
   compatibility/drift question and an explanation of the decision it can change.
3. Report each new result even if its partner is missing. Compare product validity,
   administration, uncached input/output, incomplete usage, setup and elapsed time
   separately. Historical comparisons may motivate a small contemporary matched
   follow-up, but cannot establish causality or universal efficiency.
4. Predeclare subject and aggregate stop envelopes using available observations
   and explicit uncertainty; 100,000 is demonstrably not sufficient for every old
   subject. Do not infer a safe compact cap from source-byte reduction. This design
   deliberately has no invented replacement numeric budget or reusable approval.

No frozen WI-0239 process may be resumed. A future execution needs fresh approved
scope, source/runtime/seed checks and a new unconsumed protocol. The existing
runner still stops globally on incomplete usage. This document does not implement
safe continuation, bypass that guard, or claim that two future subjects will finish.

## Stop and reuse decision cases

| Case | Required disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Completed, compatible historical row | Reuse as historical evidence; never count it as a newly executed subject |
| Changed reporting/evaluator, valid retained product | Offline recheck may create a separate observation; preserve original score and usage |
| Budget stop, no final accounting | Preserve observed lower bound; stop further dispatch under current safety policy |
| Budget stop with trustworthy final accounting and cleanup | A future explicitly qualified continuation policy may retain a censored result and continue independent work; current protocol grants no such permission |
| Confirmed isolated product failure with complete usage/cleanup | Preserve result and use the existing approved typed continuation policy |
| Unknown cleanup, source drift or instrument failure | Shared stop; do not retry or disguise as a low model score |
| Missing compact result | Measure compact if separately authorized; do not rerun known controls merely to fill a rectangular table |
| No retrievable initial seed or incompatible acceptance | Resolve offline if possible; otherwise record the specific missing evidence before proposing new work |

These cases are acceptance criteria for the next protocol review, not a new
runtime implementation. Current-policy boundary checks and actual Learning retrieval
are recorded in [verification](verification.md).

## Learning that changes this plan

Project-local Lessons record (a) repeated comparison loss when a shared stop blocks
later subjects, and (b) evidence conservation does not require wholesale reruns.
One active Practice combines them into a short experiment-preparation procedure:
inventory known evidence, label compatibility, identify the missing decision,
check the stop/coverage cases, and justify every new subject. Candidate/unknown
guidance does not become authority. No existing unrelated candidate is promoted.

Activation is intentionally approved by this work and validated against WI-0236,
WI-0239 and WI-0234's counterexample (a completed batch). The lesson is not "never
stop" or "always reuse old measurements." Review applicability when the task,
provider/measurement contract or experiment runner changes; use scheduled review
only as a reminder, not proof that guidance remains true until that date.

## Completion boundary

Finish with the inventory, scoped Learning, verified retrieval, offline boundary
evidence and a reviewed PR. No new model subjects. The next live experiment remains
unexecuted and requires the concrete compatibility review and authorization above.
