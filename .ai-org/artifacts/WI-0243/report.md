# Baseline identity repair and next discussion

## What changed

The repository-only continuity assessor now resolves both recorded baseline
references to commit objects before comparing them with the frozen full ID.
Equivalent full IDs, unique abbreviations, branch refs and annotated tags pass.
Wrong/missing/ambiguous/non-commit/option-shaped references do not. Ambiguous names
remain rejected even if repository configuration suppresses ambiguity warnings.
Candidate identity, ancestry, receipts, authority and product checks are unchanged.

Positive test records come from real claim and finish CLI operations, including
their unchanged short/named baseline strings. Explicit invalid-record controls
exercise each field separately and assert that product execution never starts.
The changes stay in the research instrument and tests, not the installed framework
or its claim API. This is not a new model-routing or Learning behavior.

Verification: full local suite **791/791 passed**; distinct
[Independent QA](independent-qa.md) accepted the exact candidate after its own
22/22 focused checks. See [verification](verification.md) for revisions and limits.

## Proposed improvement direction — not implemented here

1. **Treat small-task overhead as the next product question.** The current stable
   observation still used about twice the Operational Tokens of the most recent
   ordinary historical reference. Separate unavoidable product work from context
   acquisition and organization recording, then change one proven redundant step
   at a time. Do not assume another Position or more rules are the answer.
2. **Repair visibility before causal attribution.** Twelve of fourteen command
   items in WI-0242 were classified unknown. That is a limit of the classifier,
   not proof the commands were wasted. Prefer generation-free replay against
   retained structured events where available; do not invent per-command Tokens
   or turn missing telemetry into zero. Any classifier work needs separate scope.
3. **Measure the missing question, not every historical control again.** A later
   explicitly approved changed-spec compact subject could test recovery of changed
   authority. This repair alone cannot answer it. Keep compatible historical
   references and censoring qualifications; no new batch has been prepared here.
4. **Keep Learning promotion deliberate.** The proposed loop can eventually turn
   observed failures into reviewable candidates and regression cases. Do not mix
   unimplemented automation into the compact-instruction experiment or claim that
   this manual repair proves autonomous learning.

The next conversation should choose a bounded improvement and its acceptance
criterion before more model experiments. The current deliverable is the repaired,
tested instrument and its retained-case evidence, not an efficiency victory.
