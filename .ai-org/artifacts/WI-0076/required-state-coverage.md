# Required-state coverage — Team multi-human governance

The implementation and tests must cover:

- Solo implicit accountable person and no explicit Principal records.
- Collaborative active, suspended, and inactive Principals, including duplicate display names.
- Sponsored and unsponsored Agents without inferring online presence.
- Default Assignment versus several qualified Position pool members.
- Provisional, active, suspended, expired, and revoked membership labels.
- Temporary Bootstrap Owner, retired bootstrap, active and expired grants, invalid recovery threshold, and ready recovery quorum.
- Automated, simulated, real Collaborative, representative pilot, and High-Assurance validation states.
- Developer versus Independent QA safeguard.
- Private-viewer redaction of Principals, sponsorships, grants, and local binding.
- Desktop responsibility lanes, compact layout, 390 px mobile stacking, keyboard tabs, and reduced motion.
