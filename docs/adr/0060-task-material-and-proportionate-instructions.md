# ADR-0060: Task material and proportionate instructions

Integration note: originally numbered ADR-0059 on the local experiment branch. ADR-0059 on main is the separate proportionate-work decision; historical experiment reports retain their original numbering.

Status: accepted for user-authorized bounded implementation; unreleased.

## Context

The WI-0203 small-feature comparison completed with equal observed acceptance but higher Temple Operational Tokens and elapsed time. Entry emitted about 95–103 KB per stage. Output volume is not causal Token attribution. Reducing instruction length alone does not establish improvement.

## Decision

Keep default stage material and all current safety/authority checks. Add explicit `--material task` to separate complete relevant actor inventory records and non-current gate references from mandatory source bodies. Unknown shapes/relations and independent full-source requirements retain full material. Scope, approval, risk, policy and current evidence are not replaced by a machine-only summary. See the exact supported categories in [context routing](../extensions/context-routing.md).

Retain WI-0206's caller-attested availability as an independent option, with no persisted session cache or provider-loading claim. Bind every representation to full-source and entry digests. This read-only representation cannot satisfy a lifecycle gate or authorize mutation.

Clarify the distribution operating instructions: route optional project records by task, reuse canonical evidence rather than duplicate narratives, use the generated handoff once, and make parallel plan/prepare conditional on dispatch. Do not remove required tests, independent identities, bootstrap or human approval. Do not modify frozen experiment prompts or installed managed Skills to manufacture a cheaper result.

## Verification and limits

Test installed default/task paths, shape/requirement fallbacks, evidence categories, actor relations, freshness, source failures and reuse composition. Record both body and serialized bytes on synthetic fixtures. Full fixed-candidate verification and distinct QA are required; no model-efficiency or universal role-reduction claim follows.

Rollback is removal of the opt-in mode and restoration of the prior distribution wording. No canonical data migration or profile change is introduced.
